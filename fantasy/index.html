About & updates

Each team keeps its score in the original week and borrows the column team’s opponents. When the swapped teams originally met, their matchup is preserved so nobody plays themselves. Equal scores are ties. All-play compares each team against every other team each week; ties count as half a win.

Results are checked against Sleeper and saved every Tuesday at 6 a.m. America/Toronto, after Monday Night Football. Only weeks Sleeper marks scored and has rolled past are included. Failed or incomplete refreshes preserve the last verified report. Open pages check for saved updates every five minutes.
