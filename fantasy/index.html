    How three-year schedule luck works

    For each week, compare a manager’s score with every other score in that season. A higher score counts 1; an equal score counts ½. Divide by the number of possible opponents: seven in 2024, nine in 2025 and 2026. Add these weekly values to get expected wins.

    Schedule luck = actual wins − expected wins. Actual ties count as half a win. Add unrounded values across the seasons each person played. Positive means more wins than expected; negative means fewer. The per-14-games view divides total luck by games played and multiplies by 14. It is a comparison rate, not a forecast or an extra season.

    This measures the effect of weekly opponents on results. It does not measure injuries, lineup choices, or every source of fantasy luck. Select a manager to see the calculation for each season.

    Coverage and calculations

    Regular-season games only. Each matchup appears once. Points stay in their original season and week. People are linked by confirmed Sleeper account IDs, so team and username changes do not split a record. Missing seasons are not counted as losses.

    2024 uses corroborated weekly matchup scores. Win-loss records match saved standings, but some saved season point totals differ. Combined records and schedule luck use the weekly games.

    The league had eight teams in 2024 and ten in 2025 and 2026. The current season includes completed weeks only. Point averages use games played; different years can have different opponents and league conditions.

    About & updates

    Each team keeps its score in the original week and borrows the column team’s opponents. When the swapped teams originally met, their matchup is preserved so nobody plays themselves. Equal scores are ties. All-play compares each team against every other team each week; ties count as half a win.

    Results are checked against Sleeper and saved every Tuesday at 6 a.m. America/Toronto, after Monday Night Football. Only weeks Sleeper marks scored and has rolled past are included. Failed or incomplete refreshes preserve the last verified report. Open pages check for saved updates every five minutes.
